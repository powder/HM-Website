Who we are

Racing is better together

Her Motorsport started with a simple idea: sim racing should feel welcoming. We're a community built around women and gender-diverse drivers, open to anyone who shares our values and wants to race hard, race fair, and have a laugh doing it.

  • Multiple racing divisions — including our equal-car Spec Racing division
  • Practice sessions and friendly coaching for newer drivers
  • Active moderation and a clear Code of Conduct
  • Streamed race nights, driver profiles, and supportive community chat
What we stand for

Our paddock values

Everyone belongs

Every gender, identity, background and skill level. You don't have to prove anything to earn a spot on the grid.

Clean racing

Give room, hold your line, own your mistakes. We'd rather finish side by side than win by a punt.

Level grids

Fixed setups in our Spec division mean the fastest driver wins, and the best way to get faster is together.

Learn out loud

Ask the "silly" question. Share replays. Our experienced drivers love helping someone find a tenth.

Safe space

Zero tolerance for harassment, on track or off. Reports are taken seriously and handled privately.

Have fun

It's a hobby. Celebrate the midfield battles, the comeback drives and the first clean lap.

Questions

FAQ

Who can join?

Her Motorsport centres women, trans and non-binary drivers, and welcomes allies who share our values. If you'll treat people with respect, there's a seat for you.

I'm brand new to sim racing. Is that okay?

Absolutely. The Rookie Cup exists for exactly that, and our practice nights are low-key. Plenty of members ran their first ever online race with us.

What sim and hardware do I need?

Platform details TBD. Any wheel works, and a controller is fine for getting started.

What does "spec racing" mean?

Everyone in our Spec Racing division drives the same car with the same fixed setup. It keeps grids close and puts the focus on driving skill and racecraft. We also host other open and endurance events.

How do I report a problem?

Message any moderator privately. Every report is reviewed, and you'll never be penalised for raising a concern in good faith. Read our Code of Conduct.

Paddock rules

Code of Conduct

Short version: be kind, race clean, and look out for each other. The paddock is for everyone.

Who this applies to

Every member, guest, streamer and moderator, everywhere Her Motorsport gathers: on track, in voice and text chat, on stream, and in DMs with people you met here.

Respect everyone

  • Use people's names and pronouns. If you get it wrong, correct yourself and move on.
  • No harassment or discrimination based on gender, gender identity, sexuality, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, body or experience level.
  • No unwanted attention. This is a racing community, not a dating app.
  • Keep it welcoming for newcomers. Gatekeeping, mocking slow laps or hardware, and "well actually" lectures aren't our style.

Race clean

  • Leave a car's width. Don't divebomb, brake-check or weave to block.
  • If you cause contact, give the place back when it's safe.
  • Rejoin the track safely after an off, and check your mirrors.
  • Keep post-race discussions about incidents calm. Use the incident report process instead of arguing in chat.

Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, message any moderator privately. Reports are confidential and handled promptly. You will never be penalised for a good-faith report.

Consequences

Depending on severity, moderators may give a warning, a race penalty, a temporary suspension or a permanent removal. Serious harassment can lead to an immediate ban.
