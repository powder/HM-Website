Paddock Ethos

Racing is better together

Her Motorsport started with a straightforward goal: sim racing should feel welcoming, respectful, and fiercely competitive without the toxicity.

We're an organized community built to support, elevate, and celebrate women and gender-diverse racers. Whether you race on a desk clamp with a gamepad or a direct-drive motion rig, our doors are open.

  • Organized championship divisions where racecraft and driver dedication lead
  • Casual open practice nights with supportive coaching and line tips
  • Zero-tolerance harassment policy and active, transparent stewarding
  • Streamed broadcast races, driver spotlights, and supportive voice comms
Our Standards

What we stand for

The principles that govern our track, our voice channels, and our community.

01

Everyone Belongs

Every gender identity, background, and pace. You don't have to prove your lap time to earn respect in this paddock.

02

Hard Racing, Mutual Respect

We race hard and fight for position—that's what makes competition thrilling. Defend your line, attack for apexes, and battle wheel-to-wheel with spatial awareness. Hard racing produces close calls, but reactionary blocking, retaliatory contact, and reckless divebombs have no place on our grid.

03

Accessible Competition

No gatekeeping, no gear snobbery. Whether you race on a desk clamp with a gamepad or a direct-drive motion chassis across any of our supported simulators, dedication and racecraft lead the pack.

04

Learn Out Loud

Ask questions without fear of mockery. Share brake markers, replay angles, and setup knowledge freely.

05

Safe Space

Zero tolerance for harassment, creeps, or gatekeeping. Private reporting and impartial stewarding protect our members.

06

Midfield Glory

It's about having fun. We celebrate the comeback drives, the clean passes, and the personal bests just as loudly as the wins.

Got Questions?

Paddock FAQ

Everything you need to know about getting on track with Her Motorsport.

Who can join Her Motorsport?

Her Motorsport centres women, transgender, and non-binary drivers, and warmly welcomes allies who share our values. If you treat everyone in the paddock with genuine respect and race clean, there is a seat for you.

I'm brand new to sim racing. Is that okay?

Absolutely. The Rookie Cup exists specifically for new racers. Our weekly practice lobbies have zero stopwatch judgment, and experienced drivers are on hand to talk through lines, braking points, and car setups.

What equipment or simulator do I need?

Any force-feedback wheel or even a standard gamepad controller works for getting started. We organize events across iRacing, Assetto Corsa, and ACC—check event listings on our schedule to see which platform is being run for each round.

What does "Spec Racing" mean?

Everyone in our flagship Spec Racing division drives the exact same car with a locked, fixed setup. This eliminates any "pay-to-win" or tuning advantage, ensuring grids are close and competition highlights driving technique and racecraft.

How are incidents or bad driving reported?

We use a calm, private incident review process. Instead of arguing in race chat, drivers submit replay clips to the stewarding team. Every incident is reviewed impartially, and you will never be penalized for raising a concern in good faith.

Paddock Rules

Code of Conduct

Short version: be kind, race clean, and look out for each other. The paddock is for everyone.

Who this applies to

Every member, guest, streamer, and moderator, everywhere Her Motorsport gathers: on track, in voice channels, community text channels, Twitch streams, and in direct messages with fellow paddock members.

Respect everyone

  • Use people's names and pronouns. If you make a mistake, apologize, correct yourself, and move on.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination based on gender identity, sexuality, race, ethnicity, disability, age, or experience level.
  • No unwanted romantic or flirtatious attention. This is a sim racing community, not a dating app.
  • Keep it welcoming for newcomers. No gatekeeping, no gear snobbery, and no mocking slower drivers.

Racing Standards (iRacing Competition Principles)

We encourage fierce, committed competition and fighting hard for position. That competition relies on mutual spatial awareness and trust:

  • Fight hard, fight fair: You are encouraged to challenge for every corner and defend your position cleanly. Reactionary blocking (moving in reaction to an overtaking car) and moving under braking are strictly prohibited.
  • Spatial awareness & racing room: When an attacking car establishes significant overlap before the turn-in point, leave a car's width of racing space on track. Respect that other drivers are pushing for the same apex.
  • Safe rejoins: Following an off-track or spin, hold your brakes until completely stationary. Never roll or throttle across the racing line into oncoming traffic. Check relative timing deltas and mirrors, and rejoin parallel to the track only when safe.
  • Incidents in battle: Hard racing on the limit inevitably produces close calls and racing incidents. Do not retaliate or vent in race comms. Keep comms clear during the race and file a calm ticket with the stewarding panel afterward.

Reporting & Stewarding

If something makes you uncomfortable, message any moderator or steward privately. All inquiries are kept strictly confidential and handled promptly.

Consequences

Depending on severity, consequences range from official warnings and grid penalties to temporary suspensions or permanent disqualifications. Severe harassment results in an immediate permanent ban.
