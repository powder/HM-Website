Code of Conduct

Short version: be kind, race clean, and look out for each other. The longer version is below.

Who this applies to

Every member, guest, streamer and moderator, everywhere Her Motorsport gathers: on track, in voice and text chat, on stream, and in DMs with people you met here.

Respect everyone

  • Use people's names and pronouns. If you get it wrong, correct yourself and move on.
  • No harassment or discrimination based on gender, gender identity, sexuality, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, body or experience level.
  • No unwanted attention. This is a racing community, not a dating app.
  • Keep it welcoming for newcomers. Gatekeeping, mocking slow laps or hardware, and "well actually" lectures aren't our style.

Race clean

  • Leave a car's width. Don't divebomb, brake-check or weave to block.
  • If you cause contact, give the place back when it's safe.
  • Rejoin the track safely after an off, and check your mirrors.
  • Keep post-race discussions about incidents calm. Use the incident report process instead of arguing in chat.

Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, message any moderator privately. Reports are confidential and handled promptly. You will never be penalised for a good-faith report.

Consequences

Depending on severity, moderators may give a warning, a race penalty, a temporary suspension or a permanent removal. Serious harassment can lead to an immediate ban.
